## Company recognition, March 2026

Matt received the company's **Accelerator award** for the value "Act Like
You Own the Place."

What the award is, from the programme's own description: a corporate
initiative sponsored by human resources and developed with the culture
team, in which roughly **ten employees a month, company-wide and below
director level**, are recognised for their work and how it reflects the
company's core values. Recipients are announced during the monthly
all-employee webcast and then published on the company intranet. Anyone can
nominate, through an open form.

The specifics: a March 2026 recipient, announced at the April 2026
all-employee webcast, published on the intranet in early May with twelve
recipients that month. He was nominated by his team's product manager, who
works with the team daily but does not report to him and is not his
manager. The published nomination names five of the company's core values
and one product outcome, the AI email engagement feature.

**What it is evidence of:** third-party observation, from the product side
rather than the engineering side, of the manager competencies a ticket
tracker cannot measure, accountability, prioritisation, transparency, and
holding a high bar with empathy. **What it is not:** a peer-engineering or
director-level award, and not a performance rating. It is corroboration,
not a headline.

## The Emerging Leaders programme, 2025

Matt was nominated by his director in March 2025 and selected for the 2025
cohort of the company's Emerging Leaders programme, which he completed in
November 2025.

**What the programme is,** in the company's own description: a seven-session
virtual workshop series for people ready to grow personally and
professionally, covering leading without a title, emotional intelligence,
empathy, adapting to different personalities and communication styles,
giving and receiving feedback, and presenting yourself with impact. Sessions
are interactive and attendance at all of them is required. It wraps up with
a one-to-one with a senior leader and a final video reflection. An earlier
cohort description puts it more plainly: it is designed for high-performing
individual contributors who may have what it takes to lead a team, and
covers the transition from individual contributor to leader of people.

**Why not every new manager is in it**, from the mechanics rather than a
written policy: it is by nomination and selection rather than open
enrollment; the cohort is small and company-wide, 23 participants across
sales, client care, operations, and engineering, of whom two were from his
engineering organisation; sessions ran in two groups of about a dozen; and
full attendance is a hard requirement, with anyone unable to commit held
for the following year. Matt does not claim a specific acceptance rate.

**The timing.** The sessions ran monthly from April to September 2025. He
became a manager in May, one month in, so the programme ran alongside his
first six months of managing.

**The session sequence:** leading without a title, emotional intelligence,
empathy, positioning yourself for growth, feedback, goal setting, and
presentations.

**The capstone**, delivered in October 2025 in nine minutes twenty-five
seconds: a talk on the bus-factor problem, single points of failure in
knowledge rather than in code, and what to do about it. Its argument:
identify the single points of failure; pair subject-matter experts with at
least one other engineer and require two reviews in those areas; document
the *why* in runbooks, READMEs, diagrams, and decision records; and name
shadow engineers who can take ownership gradually, framed to the experts as
career growth rather than replacement. The facilitator's written feedback
praised the engaging opening, the practical topic, the confident delivery,
the use of data, and the timing, and recommended lighter slides, more
visuals, and defining the term earlier.

**Why it matters beyond the certificate:** the capstone's thesis became the
team's operating practice within a year. The on-call knowledge base with its
incident documents and permanent runbooks, the decision logs in every project
folder, the subject-matter plugin system, and the two-reviewer adversarial
review workflow are the remedies from that deck, implemented.

The final phase paired him with a director from a different part of the
business for a one-to-one on their leadership journey, and he submitted the
reflection video in November 2025.
